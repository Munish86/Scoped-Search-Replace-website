This policy covers this website and the Scoped Search & Replace WordPress plugin. In short: the plugin collects nothing, and this website collects only what you send us through the contact form.

The plugin

Scoped Search & Replace runs entirely inside your own WordPress site. It makes no requests to us or to anyone else, has no tracking, and sends no data out of your site. It reads and writes only your own database, when an administrator runs a search or a replacement.

The only thing it stores is the result of the last search or replacement, for the user who ran it, for 5 minutes. It is kept in your own database (or object cache) and deleted after it is shown, or when the plugin is deleted.

This website

  • No cookies for visitors, no analytics, no advertising. Fonts and images are served from this site; pages load nothing from other companies.
  • Downloads are served directly. We do not record who downloads the plugin.
  • Contact form. When you send a message we store your email address, your name, your website and the message (if you fill them in), the topic, the page you sent it from and the time. We use them only to answer you. To limit spam, we keep a count of messages per connection for one hour, using a one-way coded version of your IP address; the IP address itself is not stored.

How long we keep it

Contact messages are kept for as long as needed to answer you and follow up, and deleted within 12 months after the conversation ends.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you sent us. Write to [sr_support_email] and we will do it within 30 days.
